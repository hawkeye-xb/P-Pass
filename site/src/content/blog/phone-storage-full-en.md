The storage warning pops up, the apps and caches are already cleared, and the camera roll is all that's left. Scrolling back to an old batch of photos, you can't say whether they were ever copied to a laptop, and the ones sent through a group chat came back compressed. So they stay.

P-Pass backs up the photos on your phone to the computer at home, as original files in a folder you picked. After a one-time pairing by QR code, new photos go over automatically whenever the computer is on and the two devices can connect.

## Check what is covered first

The P-Pass home screen shows one line, "Phone 1,234 · backed up 1,180 · waiting 54", and the time of the last success. Those numbers only cover the albums selected in P-Pass's album list. If only the camera album is selected, screenshots and images saved from chats have never been sent. A photo permission of "Selected photos" instead of "Allow all" also keeps P-Pass from reading anything outside that selection.

So check that the photos you want to delete are in a selected album, then wait for the waiting count to reach zero. Right after setup, every photo already in those albums has to go over first. If the computer is turned off or Wi-Fi drops halfway, the backup resumes where it stopped, and photos that already arrived are not sent again.

Once it reads zero, check the folder on the computer: the originals are there as ordinary files that open with a double-click, without going through P-Pass.

## What deleting changes

A photo deleted on the phone stays on the computer. Backup only adds files there.

The Photos page in the phone app still shows everything on the computer and can save any of it back to the phone's gallery. Other phones paired to the same computer share that library, so your family still sees what you backed up after you delete it from your phone.

To remove photos from the computer, delete them in the folder; P-Pass records files moved or deleted there and does not send them back.

This is one copy on one computer, and if that disk fails, the backup is gone with it. For a second copy, put the folder on an external drive or another machine as well.
