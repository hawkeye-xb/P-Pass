# App Store Distribution Exception

[中文](APP_STORE_EXCEPTION.zh.md)

P-Pass is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3
(see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). This file adds a statement about distribution
through mobile app stores. It does not change the text of the license.

## The conflict

The terms of service of some app stores, such as Apple's App Store, put
limits on the people who install an app (for example, limits on the number
of devices or on redistribution). Section 10 of the AGPL does not allow a
distributor to add restrictions like these. Because of this, distributing
an app under the AGPL through such a store may be seen as a violation of
the license, even when the distributor otherwise complies with it fully.

## Our commitment

The copyright holders of P-Pass will not enforce the AGPL against anyone
for a violation that arises **only** from this conflict. The commitment
applies when a person or organisation distributes P-Pass, or a work based
on P-Pass, through an app store whose terms of service conflict with the
AGPL.

## Conditions

This commitment applies only if the distributor complies with the AGPL in
every other respect. In particular, the distributor must:

- make the Corresponding Source of the distributed app available, as
  sections 6 and 13 of the AGPL require;
- give users the full license text and this statement, or a link to them;
- keep the copyright and license notices intact.

## Scope

- The commitment covers P-Pass itself and works based on it, including
  apps published by people other than the P-Pass maintainers.
- It covers only the conflict described above. It grants no other
  exception to the AGPL.
- It is given by the copyright holders of P-Pass. Contributors make the
  same commitment for their contributions when they contribute under
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). It does not cover third-party
  components that are under their own licenses.

This statement follows the approach of the
[Nextcloud iOS app](https://github.com/nextcloud/ios/blob/master/COPYING.iOS).
